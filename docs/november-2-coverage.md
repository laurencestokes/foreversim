# November 2 launch tuning

The November 4 launch projection combines [upstream master `92c4c35b9`](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/commit/92c4c35b9e2364200ffb9e0f24573fb4904fac8f), Hunter #848, Feral #823 and the retained fork rules below. The [Build Arena method](build-arena.md) lists the exact source pins and distinguishes changes already in master from additional patch sources. [Blizzard’s notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696/9)

This report describes the implemented model and its known limits. The source records beside each result identify its simulation engine and computation date. A reused score keeps its original source; a newer compatibility check does not change that date.

## Sources and interpretation

Class, shared and item captures use game build 1.60.1.70755 with hotfix push 113605. The racial capture remains on build 1.60.1.70707 with push 113562. The latest regeneration changed capture stamps and group assignments without changing spell rows. Shaman's active spell data comes from the current captures and the explicit assumptions in its implementation. [Captured spell inputs](../assets/db_inputs/spell_store_inputs.json) · [Feral coefficient overrides](../tools/database/overrides/spell_overrides.go) · [Overload rules](../sim/shaman/electric_spell.go) · [Chain Lightning rolls](../sim/shaman/chain_lightning.go)

The bear model retains #822's threat assumptions: 2.15 times threat in bear form, 1.75 for Maul and Swipe, and 3 for Primal Bite. These values still need in-game verification. They do not establish tank balance. [Bear form](../sim/druid/forms.go) · [Maul](../sim/druid/maul.go) · [Swipe](../sim/druid/swipe.go) · [Primal Bite](../sim/druid/primal_bite.go)

An older saved Rogue, Warrior or Shaman talent string keeps its surviving ranks by talent name. Removed talents free their points, and ranks above a new limit are reduced. An imported build can need points reassigned. All ranked builds for these classes are checked for prerequisites and 51 allocated points. The two original Subtlety alternatives share talents and use different builders.

The Rogue rotation uses Shank at 80 Energy or less. With Boot Knife, it also waits for three or fewer combo points. All eleven Rogue presets wear four pieces of the original Darkmantle set and receive its Energy proc. Riposte and Ghostly Strike's dodge bonus belong to tank setups; the ranked Rogue fights place the player behind the target. [Recorded rotation](../ui/specs/rogue/dps/apls/default.apl.json) · [Shank](../sim/rogue/shank.go) · [Death Mark](../sim/rogue/death_mark.go)

The major DPS racials in this post were already in the engine. Dwarf has 1% physical and spell hit. Human has 1% crit and 10% Spirit. Orc has its pet damage bonus and two-minute Blood Fury. Skyborne has 2% haste, Troll Berserking lasts 15 seconds, and Undead Touch of the Grave has a 15-second internal cooldown. Tauren Endurance gives 5% health. The Tauren Warrior’s base Strength is five points above the Human Warrior’s. Skyborne base attributes still use earlier observations. The raid presets use ordinary Read Leyline and Skysight. [Racial effects](../sim/core/racials.go) · [Base stats](../sim/core/base_stats.go)

The 41 level-60 builds fight one target for 90, 180 or 360 seconds against Mechanical, Elemental and Beast creature types. Hunter's Dragonkin default on its class page does not change that matrix. The November 4 selection recomputes 29 builds and reuses 12 after input, code and bounded differential checks. Reused measurements retain their original 50,000 iterations, engine revisions and dates. [Selection and checks](november-4-arena-reuse.md)

Each build keeps its equipment, talents, rotation and support across races. Gear quality differs between builds, and some replacement items have not been optimized. Rankings measure personal DPS, including pets. They exclude mana or damage support supplied to other players, survival and utility. Healing and tank performance, PvP, leveling and encounter-imposed movement are outside this comparison. Hunter's programmed movement remains part of its own rotation.
